---
title: The Quiet Virtues
date: 2025-11-12
excerpt: Patience, attention, and restraint — the dispositions that resist measurement.
---

Some virtues announce themselves. Courage has a moment; generosity has a gesture. Others do
their work by not happening — the sharp reply withheld, the interruption declined, the
judgement postponed one more day.

These are hard to teach because they are hard to point at. They show up in the negative
space of a life, and the only honest evidence for them is a long stretch of time in which
someone reliably did not do the easier thing.

I suspect this is also why they fare badly in any culture that measures. What cannot be
counted eventually stops being cultivated, and we lose the dispositions we had no way of
noticing we were losing.
